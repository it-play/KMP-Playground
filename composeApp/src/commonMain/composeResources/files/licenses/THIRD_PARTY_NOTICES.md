## Opening slideshow photographs

The opening contains 100 real photographs of exchanges, trading floors,
financial districts, currency markets, and market history. Detailed
per-file attribution is distributed with the application in
`files/licenses/OPENING_PHOTO_CREDITS.csv`. Each row identifies the packaged
resource, title, author, year, license and license URL, original Wikimedia
Commons file page, and the modifications made for display.

The packaged copies were converted to sRGB, resized as needed to a maximum
1600-pixel edge, recompressed as JPEG at quality 84, and stripped of embedded
metadata. The application may center-crop them at runtime to fit the display.

Modified photograph files whose CSV entry names a Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike license are distributed under that same license. This
ShareAlike declaration applies only to each identified photograph file; it
does not change the license of the application code, logos, or other assets.
Public-domain, CC0, CC BY, and Korea Open Government License Type 1 items
remain available under the terms identified in their corresponding CSV rows.
